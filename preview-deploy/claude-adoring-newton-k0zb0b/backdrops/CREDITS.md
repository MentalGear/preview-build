## raschpetzer-qanat.jpg

Cropped and downscaled from:

- **Title:** Walferdange, Grünewald, Raschpëtzer (Puits -04 intérieur).jpg
- **Subject:** Interior of a shaft ("puits") of the Raschpëtzer Qanat, a Gallo-Roman
  underground aqueduct near Walferdange, Luxembourg (built c. 150 AD).
- **Author:** GilPe
- **Source:** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Walferdange,_Gr%C3%BCnewald,_Raschp%C3%ABtzer_(Puits_-04_int%C3%A9rieur).jpg)
- **License:** [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- **Changes:** re-cropped square and tight to the shaft's warm sandstone spiral —
  excluding the modern equipment (light, cable, survey box) at the base of the shaft
  and most of the grey concrete access collar at the rim, so the crop reads as
  abstract aged stone rather than a literal manhole photo once blurred — downscaled
  to 900px, re-encoded as JPEG.
