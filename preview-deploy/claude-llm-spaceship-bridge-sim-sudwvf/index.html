How this works. A deterministic simulation owns the ship — physics, damage, weapons, sensors. Claude never flies the Meridian; it reads your order plus the current state, then answers with the officers' lines and one structured action the page validates and applies. Impossible orders fail in the simulation and the officer says so.
Voice. Officers speak through your browser's own speech synthesiser, so their replies cost nothing and are instant. Speech input uses the browser's recogniser where it is available — Chrome, Edge and Safari — and the typed order line always works. Damage callouts are spoken from templates the moment the simulation raises them, never waiting on a model.
